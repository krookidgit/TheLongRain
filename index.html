Milestone 1 · HearthThe Long Rain

The sky broke three hundred years ago. Carry an ember of the Undying Hearth into the Wyldwood, keep three peoples content, and win the Crown's favour before its patience runs out.

  • Drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap anything to inspect it.
  • Fill Renown to 12 before the Crown's Patience reaches 12.
  • Keep the hearth fed. In the Tempest only buildings in hearth range work.
  • Every tree you fell angers the wood. High Ire makes Tempests worse.
